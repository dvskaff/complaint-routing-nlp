# Model card: complaint router

_Generated by the pipeline on 2026-11-08 03:32 UTC. Numbers are on held-out test months._

## What it does
Reads the free-text narrative of a consumer financial complaint and predicts which of 11 product
areas it belongs to (the CFPB's product categories), with a calibrated confidence. A routing policy sends confident
predictions straight to the product team and the rest to human review.

## Model
- Base: `sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2` (6-layer MiniLM, 22M parameters), fine-tuned end to end with a classification head.
- Input: the first 256 tokens of the narrative (after removing the CFPB's XXXX redaction marks).
- Training: 16,500 complaints from 2025-08 to 2026-04, at most
  1,500 per product; 3 epochs, AdamW, learning rate 8e-05, batch
  32; best epoch by validation macro-F1 (epoch 3).
- Calibration: prior correction for the real product mix + temperature scaling (T = 1.10),
  fitted on 2026-05.
- Deployment format: ONNX, 8-bit dynamic quantization, 23 MB, calibration inside the graph;
  98.0% top-1 agreement with PyTorch on 400 validation complaints.

## Intended use and limits
- Intended for triage support: suggesting a team and flagging uncertain complaints for a person. Not for deciding
  outcomes for consumers.
- Labels are the product the consumer selected when filing, so some disagreement is labeling noise, not model error.
- English narratives only; long narratives are truncated; products with fewer than
  300 training complaints are not predicted.
- The CFPB stopped publishing narratives in August 2026, so the data ends there. A live deployment would need fresh
  labeled complaints and drift monitoring.
- Narratives are public, consumer-consented and redacted by the CFPB; the model is not trained to extract personal data.
